Master UI/UX Screen List
1. Workshop Management System
#	UI screen	What it shows
1.1	Workshop Owner Dashboard	Today’s jobs, bookings, revenue, pending approvals, workshop activity
1.2	Job Cards List	All job cards, vehicle, customer, status, advisor, date
1.3	Create New Job Card	Search vehicle number, customer details, vehicle details
1.4	Vehicle Check-in & Inspection	Vehicle condition, checklist, photos, customer concerns
1.5	Job Card Details	Complete job overview, tasks, estimates, bills, activity
1.6	Workshop Workflow Board	Jobs grouped by workflow status
1.7	Task Management	Tasks, assigned technicians, deadlines, progress
1.8	Mechanic Task Details	Assigned work, instructions, photos, status updates
1.9	Estimate Builder	Labour, parts, charges, taxes, estimated total
1.10	Final Bill	Completed work, final charges, payment status
1.11	Service Booking Management	Booking calendar, requests, confirmations, cancellations
1.12	Customer & Vehicle Records	Customer profiles, vehicles, service history
1.13	Pickup & Drop Management	Pickup requests, addresses, timings, status
1.14	Customer Communication	WhatsApp messages, updates, reminders
1.15	Workshop Reports	Jobs, revenue, services, technician performance

2. Inventory & Parts Management
#	UI screen	What it shows
2.1	Inventory Dashboard	Stock value, low-stock items, pending requests
2.2	Parts Catalogue	Searchable parts list with categories and vehicle compatibility
2.3	Part Details	SKU, stock, price, supplier, movement history
2.4	Parts Requests	Parts requested for job cards
2.5	Issue Parts to Job Card	Requested parts, available stock, issue quantity
2.6	Purchase Orders	Orders placed with suppliers
2.7	Receive Stock	Received quantities, invoice details, discrepancies
2.8	Stock Movements	Stock in, stock out, adjustments, returns
2.9	Supplier Management	Supplier profiles, contact details, purchase history
2.10	Inventory Reports	Stock valuation, usage, slow-moving and low-stock parts

3. Parts Vendor Network
#	UI screen	What it shows
3.1	Parts Network Search	Search parts across participating suppliers
3.2	Parts Comparison	Supplier, price, availability, delivery estimate
3.3	Request a Quote	Select part and send enquiry to suppliers
3.4	Quote Responses	Compare supplier quotations
3.5	Order & Tracking	Order status and delivery progress
3.6	Vendor Dashboard	Vendor catalogue, quotations, orders
3.7	Vendor Product Management	Add products, prices, stock availability

4. Tools & Equipment Vendor Network
#	UI screen	What it shows
4.1	Equipment Marketplace	Tools and equipment by category
4.2	Equipment Details	Images, specifications, price or quote option
4.3	Compare Equipment	Compare products and specifications
4.4	Request Equipment Quote	Submit requirements to vendors
4.5	Vendor Enquiry Management	Manage enquiries and responses
4.6	Equipment Vendor Dashboard	Listings, enquiries, leads

5. Graphics Design & Marketing Studio
#	UI screen	What it shows

5.5	Creative Preview & Export	Preview, download, share
5.6	Campaign Planner	Plan upcoming promotions and seasonal creatives
5.7	Social Content Library	Saved creatives and previously used content
